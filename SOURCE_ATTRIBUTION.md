MoonAttest is an original implementation. No source files were copied or translated from MoonFlow, moonbit-workflow-engine, OpenTelemetry MoonTrace, MoonBench, moon_mutest, MoonNinja, or moonguard. Those projects were inspected only at the public project-description level to identify functional overlap and establish a different scope.
